# 16_2026 · 2026 flat vector illustration + full-film punchline ending

All performance is written with **full-film frame number `F = t*60`**; key numbers are copied verbatim from `../breakdown/shot-list.md` §4, so the film can be checked frame by frame against the original.
Compare with the contact sheet (original on the left | recreation on the right): `../renders/16_2026/cmp_E1…E4_*.jpg`; the clip is `clip_2026.mp4` (10.8–15.06s).

## Style (Dribbble flat + grain)
- Flat color blocks, no outlines; forms are separated only by "one shade darker in the same color family" blocks (the sweater's lit diagonal block `#f08b7d`, the cat's thigh `#f3ad4c` plus a dark curved edge).
- **Grain stippling `stipple()`**: scatter 2px square dots inside shapes; density is a function of position — pink circle `0.12 + 0.75·clamp((y-cy+120)/420)` (denser downward), the lilac block is denser toward the lower right, the sun is denser toward the lower right. All static parts go into the cache `fl_bg`.
- After the character layer is drawn, overlay a static grain texture with `source-atop` (5% dark dots at α70, 3.5% light dots at α90), so grain lands only on the character.
- Light cone: 26,000 points, width increases linearly with y from 56 to 150, opacity falls off with y, denser near the center; cached as a 340×420 texture, swinging around (1010,0) together with the pendant lamp.

## The girl (custom poses built on the RIG)
- Torso/neck anchor: `RIG.girl({s:1.12})`; call `RIG.girl({s:1.344, dy:43.7})` again for the head — equivalent to scaling the head 1.2× around the neck point, the big-head proportion of flat illustration. Face, bangs, back hair, bun, and eye/lip/ear anchors all come from it.
- Arms: `RIG.ik2(shoulder, target, 150, 140, -1)`, blending three target sets by progress: typing (both hands alternate up and down by 7px on the keyboard at 22 rad/s) → petting the cat (688–704, the near hand draws small circles on the cat's back) → exclaiming (791–798, `ease.outBack` raises it in front of the chin, fingers spread).
- Expression: closed-eye arcs + upturned mouth (<686) → dot eyes + highlights, small o mouth (from 686, the o pops out with outBack) → big eyes + wide open mouth + tongue (from 791) → after frame 838, switch to a squinting "<" (the original's final frame is also closed-eyed).
- Tension lines: 3, radiating from above and in front of the head, length pops with outBack; color 793 pale pink → 796 full red (RGB interpolation); 830–888 random jitter ±2.5px every 2 frames, head jitters ±2px in sync.
- Trouser legs/socks/shoes drawn by hand (the RIG wears a long skirt).

## The cat (pose library + interpolation)
Each pose is a set of local key points (facing right, origin = centroid): body 10 points, white chest 6 points, thigh ellipse, 3 back-stripe segments, head, two front legs, hind leg, tail 4 Bezier points, gy (sole to centroid).
Poses: `SIT_F` (sitting on the floor, measured from the original at 10.95s), `SIT_T` (sitting on the table; measured facing left in the original, then mirrored to face right for storage), `LEAP` (stretched horizontally), `LAND` (front paws down). `mixPose` lerps each numeric value.
Transforms: `translate(centroid) → [rotate around sole or around centroid] → scale(dir·sx, sy)`; squash and stretch both pivot around the sole, so the feet stay on the ground when crouching.

## The cup and the shatter
- Cup x: linear interpolation through the 16 measured points in the storyboard table (744:981 … 796:801); starts tilting outward -0.12 at 790–796.
- Fall 796–815: Catmull-Rom through 9 measured center points (measured gravity ≈1.75px/frame²), rotation `-0.12 - 2.3·q^1.3`; the 6 coffee droplets behind it take the cup's position from 1.3-frame intervals in the past, earlier droplets smaller.
- 815 impact: 7 radial impact lines fade over 8 frames; two large shards follow Lagrange parabolas through three storyboard-table points (818/824/834), then stop after landing at 834; 9 small crumbs follow parabolas and stop at their own landing heights 893–911; coffee stain width 818:140 → 822:220 → 826:235 (ease.out); two gray ripples expand to 560/460 wide and fade out before 838; the handle half-circle + tea-bag tag remain on the floor.
- Swat motion lines: 3 short lines on the cup's right side, expanding outward and fading over 12 frames after impact.

## Secondary environment motion
- Pendant lamp: `damped(F, 686, 0.055rad, 26-frame period, 22-frame decay) + damped(F, 830, 0.045, 22, 20)`; the light cone swings with it.
- Plants: `damped(F, 697, 0.035, 22, 18)` (sway) + `damped(F, 821, 0.05, 9, 26)` (shiver, short period); each monstera leaf adds a small self-sway with a different phase.
- Clouds drift left at 15px/s (0.25px/frame); the small plane at 177px/s leaves a dashed trail (x≈440 at 12.0s, x≈750 at 13.75s, matching the original); sun rays rotate at 0.35 rad/s; 17 decorative symbols boil at 4fps (±1.5px, ±0.09rad).
