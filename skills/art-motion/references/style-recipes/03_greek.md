# 03_greek · 530 BCE Attic black-figure pottery — code drawing notes

File: `scenes/03_greek.js` (pure Canvas 2D, no images). Final self-check: warm max 15.9ms / mean 7.6ms / cold 80ms, determinism passed, 0.2↔0.7s motion differential 22% of pixels moving.

## The one-line trick
Black-figure = the **black-glaze silhouette + incised lines + added color** trio; none may be missing:
- Silhouette: every person/object filled `#1b120d` (near-black with warmth), no stroke; form comes from the silhouette's contrast against the clay ground.
- Incised lines: "cut through" the black silhouette, drawing 1.5–2px thin lines in bright clay orange `#e8904c` (drapery folds, facial features, the boundary where limbs overlap).
- Added color: purple-red `#8a2a37` bands (hairband, cuffs, hem, collar, cushion) plus small white dots (`#f6ead6`, r≈2, spacing 11–17px).
Get these three right and it reads as a Greek pottery vase at a glance, with no texture at all.

## Palette
Clay `#dc6a2f`, bright clay (vase panel) `#e8803f`, black glaze `#1b120d`, purple-red `#8a2a37`, white `#f6ead6`, incision `#e8904c`, gold hair `#f2bd48`, clay reserve (cat white area) `#f3cfa4`, cat orange `#f2913f`, cat nose `#e7897a`.

## Pipeline and parameters
1. **Clay ground (cached `gr_bg`)**: at 480×270 low resolution, per-pixel fbm(×0.016, 4 octave) mottling ±30 plus `noise(x*0.006, y*0.85)` **horizontal band noise** (wheel-throwing rings) ±9, then scaled up to 1920×1080 (low frequencies are blurry anyway, saving 16× the computation); then 560 random horizontal thin lines (length 150–1050, alpha 0.03–0.08, two kinds, dark and light) as fine throwing lines; 420 dark small dots + 120 light dots; two layers of `P.grain`; 5 cracks (each 9px per segment, ±2px jitter, with an additional 1px-offset faint bright edge along the lower side so the crack immediately has thickness). One warm radial highlight 0.16 overlaid at the center. Cold-start full frame ~80ms.
2. **Vignette (cached `gr_vig`)**: radial 380→1180, outer edge rgba(30,6,0,.62), the final layer covering the whole frame (including the bands).
3. **Static black-figure layer (cached `gr_static`)**: picture frame (18px black border + white dot string at 11.6px spacing + inner thin line), grapevine (sine stem + triangular grape cluster of 3-2-1 dots), table (legs with beast-claw feet), turned chair legs (width table [y,w] polyline shaped symmetrically), swan-neck chair back (13px thick stroked curve + oval head + triangular beak), amphora (24-point symmetric outline→`RIG.smooth`, clay reserved on the vase panel with a small black cat, ray pattern at the base, one vertical 0.09 gloss gradient), two stretches of Greek inscription.
4. **Greek inscription**: the font stack has no Greek glyphs → hand-written **stroke table** (each letter 1–3 polyline segments, unit cell 0..1). Letter height 30, stroke width 3.4, per-letter height ×(0.9–1.06) random, baseline ±6% jitter, vertices ±0.7px jitter; Ο drawn as a small circle (0.25 of letter height, since the original film has a small o), Ι 2px wide. The hand-painted pottery inscription feel comes mostly from "unequal letter heights + the small o".
5. **Scrolling bands**: the top meander (period 60px, 7px black line, unit polyline `[[4,46],[4,0],[50,0],[50,34],[18,34],[18,14],[36,14],[36,22]]` plus a continuous bottom line) and the bottom lotus-bud chain + ray pattern (period 76px = two 38px units, odd buds filled purple-red, so the real period is 76) are each pre-rendered as a strip of width W+2P, and each frame does `drawImage(strip, -(off mod P))`. Offset curves:
   - Top: `off = 22·lt + 60·easeInOut((lt−0.49)/0.35)`, toward the left;
   - Bottom: `off = −(18·lt + 76·easeInOut((lt−0.477)/0.367))`, toward the right.
   The original film only makes one easeInOut period jump at frames 169–191; I added an extra 18–22px/s constant drift so the first 0.47s of this segment also visibly moves.
6. **Motifs inside the frame** (drawn every frame, clipped into the frame): 6 dolphins (hand-written bezier silhouettes: beak, dorsal fin, pectoral fin, forked tail + belly incision + white eye dot) swim back and forth ±9px along their own heading, angle ±0.12rad, period 0.85s, mutual phase difference 1.3; the whole boat heaves ±4px and rocks ±0.025rad about (560,420), period 0.9s. White sail + purple-red stitching, crescent hull + scorpion-tail stern, purple-red cabin, reclining figure.
7. **Lyre**: pivots about the peg (1050,150) with `0.09·sin(2π·lt/0.8+0.5)` (±5°, ±17px displacement at the soundbox, obvious within 1 second). Tortoise-shell soundbox = black circle + incised ring + 5 small incised circles.
8. **Rosette**: 8-petal black ellipses + incised petal separations + purple-red center, rotating back and forth at `lt·1.6` rad/s.
9. **Orange-and-white cat** (hand-drawn rather than the RIG default polygon; the tail uses `RIG.cat`'s tailPts following choreo.tail): the whole thing scaled 0.9 into the standard frame about (548,903); orange silhouette + 3.4px black stroke; white chest/paws/muzzle in the "clay reserve" cream; **the back tabby stripes take 12 points equally spaced by arc length along the back contour and draw tapered stripes toward the body center (575,800)** (length 30–48, root width 10) — far more natural than hand-placed stripes; thigh arc, vertical stripes on the forelegs; archaic large round eyes (white eye + black pupil + highlight + a brow arc above), swapped for an arc line when blinking; purple-red collar with white dots.
10. **Girl**: `RIG.girl({cup,sip,breathe,s:0.97,dy:-14,hair:'long'})` — scaled down and shifted so the feet land on ground line 903.
    - The body is filled directly from RIG's Path2D (farSleeve/skirt/torso/neck); the far hand is replaced with a small ellipse (RIG's farHand looks like a blade in a black silhouette).
    - **Head drawn by hand**: the Greek profile (straight nose bridge running into the forehead, fine chin) plus hair outline written in RIG's rest coordinate system, then reproducing RIG's head motion with canvas transforms: `translate(neck)·rotate(G.tilt)·translate(−neck)·T`. The same matrix is computed again with `DOMMatrix`, and `Path2D.addPath(faceSil, M)` provides the clip region for the incised lines.
    - **Gold hair** (the key to reading "the blonde girl"): inside the hair clip, 11 wavy strands over the crown (quadratic bezier + 2.6px·sin(7πu) normal ripple), 8 locks of long wavy hair down the back (sine of 4.5 half-waves + a small spiral at the tip), the bun as a double spiral, 4 small curls at the brow, two spiral curls at the temples (a 6px black underlay stroke first, then 2.2px gold on top, so they still read when they cross outside the silhouette). Gold lines need 2.5px to carry "amount" at 1080p; at 2px you cannot tell it is blonde hair in a thumbnail.
    - Incised lines: 16 drapery folds (clipped inside the body), almond eyes (light eye white + black pupil, the front eye drawn on the profile face), brow, nostril, lips, chin. **Boundary where the near arm overlaps the body/head**: clip(body∪head) and then stroke an incision along the arm contour — black-on-black can only be separated this way, and it keeps the face from smearing into one blob when the hand rises to the mouth.
    - Added color: 15px purple-red band on the hem + white dots, cuff band (clipped inside the upper arm, a 15px band along the upper-arm normal + incisions above and below), bracelet, 8 four-petal small white-dot flowers, hairband.
    - Kylix cup (wide shallow two-handled, half-width 32) follows `G.cup` (including tilt), purple-red line at the rim; the steam is three thin black "≀" lines, phase `t·9`.

## What moves (all visible within 0–0.94s)
Raising the cup (choreo.cup, 0.9s up to the mouth + head tilting back slightly), cat tail/blink/breathing, top and bottom band scrolling (drift + beat-period jump), lyre sway, boat heaving, dolphins swimming, rosette spinning, steam twisting.

## Pitfalls hit
- Scaling RIG's hair outline straight up made the back of the head too wide (in v1 the back of the head reached x=1450 and the head looked like a helmet); pulled it in to 1424 per the design sheet, with long hair hanging only to y≈558.
- With the cup at the mouth, black hand + black cup + black face smeared into one mass: ① incise a boundary between arm and head ② shrink the cup (half-width 44→32) ③ keep a purple-red line at the rim.
- RIG's cat crown reaches y=535 while the standard frame's cat top is 610; unscaled it presses into the bottom edge of the frame.
- With alternating odd/even buds the bottom band's real period is 2 units (76px); the pre-rendered strip's wrap period must be 76, or the color jumps every 38px.
- `P.grain`'s seed = key length, so the two grain layers' keys must have different lengths, otherwise the pattern is identical.
- **The cup-raising endpoint must be period-appropriate (review rework)**: RIG's cup→mouth endpoint sits near the nose tip with a cup tilt of −0.45rad; swapped to the wide shallow kylix it looked like a beak/pipe stuck on the face, and the hand still covered mouth and chin. Changed to home-made IK: the lip point rest(1243,391) is transformed to screen with the head's DOMMatrix, the rim center = lip point −(30,−1) (the rim's right end just touches the lips), tilt −0.08·cup (≤5°), the hand's IK target = rim +(lerp(32,6,cup), 30), i.e. supporting under the foot of the cup; `RIG.ik2(S, hand target, 165s, 122s, −1)` plus `RIG.taper` produces the upper arm/forearm itself. The forearm stands in front of the chest, and the profile of nose, lips and chin is fully exposed. Steam follows the rim.

## v1 review rework (cat switched to black-figure + more top-band scrolling)
- **The cat must use the same drawing system as the human**: a cartoon cat with bright orange big round eyes breaks the black-figure illusion at a glance. Changed to a black-glaze silhouette, with "orange and white" carried only by added color and incised separations: a 26px purple-red saddle band along the back contour, a purple-red patch on the crown (46×22 ellipse clipped inside the head), black tail base + 7px purple-red core; all tabby stripes are clay-orange `#f2913f` 3px **incisions** (14 curved lines toward the body center equally spaced by arc length along the back contour + 3 on the thigh + 3 on the forelegs + a ring every 2 points along the tail); white chest/muzzle/paws in white paint `#efe1c8`, with black incised toes on the paws; eyes changed to archaic incised almond eyes (white-paint eye white + vertical oval black pupil + a small incision at the outer corner), the blink being a single downward-curving incision; nose purple-red, whiskers incised on the left (landing on the black face) and black lines on the right (landing on the clay).
- **Scrolling a periodic pattern by a whole number of periods = first and last frames identical**: after one 60px-period jump the top band's image is exactly the same as before the jump, which a still comparison or frame sampling easily judges as "not moving". Constant drift raised from 22→48px/s (bottom band 18→30px/s), guaranteeing a phase difference between any two frames.
- Motion amount in the film's static stretch (480×270 mean frame difference): 03_greek 1.87 → 2.02, original film 2.06. warm max 17ms.
