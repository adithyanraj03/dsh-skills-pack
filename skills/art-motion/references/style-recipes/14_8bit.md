# 14_8bit · 1985 8-bit pixel game

## Pipeline
1. **Draw the whole image on a 240×135 low-resolution canvas** (1 low-res pixel = 8 screen pixels; measured on the original, the pixel blocks are exactly 8px), then set `imageSmoothingEnabled=false` and upscale 8× with a single `drawImage`.
2. **Per-pixel `fillRect` background** (cached `8b_bg`): cross-dot wall = 6px grid, 3×3 crosses staggered 3px on alternate rows; wood floor band y=106–113, a black seam every 24px; bricks 16×6 + 1px seams, staggered 8 on alternate rows, with a row of beige highlight along the top and left edges (`#fbe2a6`). The sky in the window uses 3 levels of blue (`#0856ef / #47c4f8 / #aae9fb`), with a `(x+y)%2` checkerboard dither band of ±0.05 at the boundaries — the only way NES does gradients.
3. **Characters = RIG paths scaled to low resolution and filled → spriteize**: each part on its own layer (far arm / body and legs / hair / face / bangs / near arm; cat body / cat head), each layer:
   - `getImageData` → alpha < 110 becomes transparent outright (removing antialiasing) → the rest quantized to a 14-color character palette (nearest color);
   - outline 1px black (a transparent pixel with an opaque pixel among its 4 neighbors is painted black).
   **Per-layer outlining is the key**: outlining only the overall outer contour once makes hair/face/arm blur into one mass; outlining each part separately is what gives NES sprites their inner contours.
4. **All features drawn pixel by pixel** (eyes 1×2 black + brows, cat eyes 2×2 black + 1 white highlight at upper left, pink nose 1px, blush 2×1), positioned by rounding RIG anchors `/8`.
5. **The mug does not use RIG.drawCup**: AA paths scaled down to 6px wide go blurry; draw a 6×5 white cup directly at low resolution + two red stripes + 3px handle, following `G.cup`.
6. **HUD drawn at full resolution**: `PressStart2P` 48px (font 1 pixel = 6 screen pixels; 64px is too wide and makes WORLD 1-1 collide with the apple icon), 8px black drop shadow; cat head/heart/apple icons are string bitmaps, 8×8 per cell.

## Jeans
RIG.girl is a long dress; the 8-bit version uses two `RIG.taper` segments (hip `G.hipSeat`→knee (1222,724)→ankle (1214,872), widths 92/76/56) to draw blue jeans, with the far leg one step darker `#0838a8` offset back 26px; white sneakers + one red stripe.

## Small loops (only about 0.23s on screen ≈ 11 visible frames, all done as stepped animation)
- Cat tail: 15fps four-pose loop `[-0.55, 0, 0.55, 0]` (RIG.cat's tail parameter), pixel animation's "a few drawings alternating".
- HUD heart blink: lt 0.16–0.23 (≈ frame 630), swapping every 2 frames: full heart turns white, empty heart turns full red.
- Potted plant shake: in the same window the leaves shift by whole pixels `[0,1,0,-1]`.
- Question block pops out a coin: over lt 0.06–0.22 a half-sine rises 9px then falls back.
- Clouds outside the window shift left 1px over 2 frames; pixel steam is three columns of 1×2 dots, moving up 1px per frame at 30fps, looping every 6px.

## Pitfalls hit
- The RIG cat's head reaches y≈535 and covers the windowsill (the window goes to y=590). This is the shared skeleton's position, so it was not moved; the 8-bit black outline makes the overlap more obvious, but transitions depend on the standard composition, so it was kept.
- `spriteize` reads unpremultiplied RGBA; do not divide by alpha again.
- Performance: hot frame 2.5–6ms, cold start 11ms.
