# 11_cubism · 1912 Analytical Cubism (Picasso / Braque)

File: `scenes/11_cubism.js` (~280 lines). Performance: warm average 13ms, max 19ms, first frame (cache build) ≈100ms; determinism passes; motion difference 6.9% of pixels from lt 0.34→0.46. On screen for 0.47s (T10 fragment transition 0.24s).

## Pipeline
1. **Fully cached base `cb_bg` — "transparent overlapping planes"**: the core function `plane(g, pts, col, alpha, r)`: one linear gradient per plane, starting at vertex 0 and ending at a randomly chosen other vertex, three color stops = 25% off-white mix → original color → 40% dark-brown mix, alpha 0.18–0.7. Compositing: 18 hand-placed large planes (following the original's composition positions) + 9 radial fan planes under the table (emanating from (1000,1090), alternating light and dark at α0.3) + 70 random 3/4-gons (avoiding the year area at upper right). Stacking many translucent gradients produces that Analytical Cubism space — "transparent, interpenetrating, each plane light on one side and dark on the other".
   - Texture planes: 4 pointillist patches (9px diamond grid, r1.6), 4 hatched patches (7px-pitch diagonals), a page of ruled paper at the left edge.
   - Braque's short-dash sand grain: 9000 small dashes 3–8×1.5px, 55% off-white α0.28 / 45% dark brown α0.16 — the original's "brick texture" relies on it.
   - 46 thin lines running through the whole image (1.3px α0.55) = passage.
   - Faux wood grain `wood()`: draw S/6 wavy lines along the long edge, add a Gaussian bump at each knot position `14·exp(-(u-k)²/0.01)`, knots as 4 concentric ellipses. Used for the tabletop and the right-side wood panel (Braque's combed fake wood). Cane chair back = 10px diamond holes + bidirectional diagonals.
2. **Window-pane fragment sliding**: the window view is cached separately as a 560×540 image (55 blue-gray facets + 3 diagonal light beams + Eiffel Tower + gull arcs); each of the four panes clips it and samples it at a different offset `dx = sin(9lt+1.7i)·18 + 10fps jitter ±3`, `dy = cos(7lt+i)·8` — the same window view offset across four panes = multi-viewpoint assembly; each pane also has a translucent triangle sliding horizontally across at 260px/s.
3. **CAFÉ stencil letters jumping and swapping places**: `AlfaSlabOne-400` 104px drawn into a small offscreen layer, 12fps on the beat (`st=floor(lt·12)`), order table `CAFÉ/ACFÉ/CAÉF/CFAÉ` cycling, each step each letter y ±13, rotation ±0.11rad (hash-derived); then `destination-out` cuts horizontal and vertical gaps = stencil letters (the gaps reveal the overlapping planes underneath, more convincing than drawing paper-colored bars).
4. **Newspaper JOU**: cache a 300×190 small image (LibreBaskerville-700 84px "JOU" + 12px headline "LA BATAILLE / S'EST ENGAGÉ UN CHAT" + three columns of gray greeking), every frame a subtle 8fps wobble (rotation ±0.0125rad, x ±1.5px), the top-right corner folding with `sin(20lt)`.
5. **Characters = hand-written faceter `facetize`**: RIG flat fill (fill mode, no lines) into an offscreen layer → read back the pixels of that bounding box → jitter a triangle mesh (random diagonal per cell) → each triangle takes the color at its centroid, `×(0.8+0.38·rand)` for value, then a bright→dark gradient from vertex 0 to vertex 2 → **triangles are not clipped** and are drawn straight back (those with centroid alpha<120 skipped). Not clipping is the key: the silhouette edge gets cut into jagged straight edges, and some triangles even extend past the contour — that is the Cubist "planes spilling out of the outline" feel.
   - Girl cell 58 (large planes); the hair area is re-cut finer with cell 26, cutting only "colors that look like golden hair" (`only: R>150 && B<130 && R-B>60`); cat cell 26.
   - Vertices jitter ±8% cell at 8fps, and 16–35% of triangles jump ±12% in value (boil) → the characters are "micro-moving", exactly what the original shows in the cat region at frames 548–562.
6. **After cutting, add the Picasso-style structure back**: repaint two skin-value planes on the face (facetizing drags golden-hair colors into the face) + a blue-gray plane over the front half of the face at α0.82 + a frontal almond eye (a frontal eye drawn on a profile face) + spiral ear + red lips; white collar as two light/dark triangles + three buttons; skirt from the waist with 8 radial cut lines + alternating light/dark wedge planes; outer contour with straight segments `segs()`, each segment extended 10–16px past both ends (lines drawn past the boundary). Cat: hexagonal straight-line head contour, `bodyPts` straight-line body contour, yellow diamond eyes + vertical pupils, triangular profile nose.
7. The topmost 5 planes at α0.2 press down on the girl and cat and shift with `sin(6lt)·8` — embedding the characters in the space instead of letting them look pasted onto the background.

## Pitfalls (important)
- **Non-determinism: the first frame differs from the rest.** Two causes stack, both appearing only on the first frame: ① the offscreen layer that needs `getImageData` is drawn on the GPU the first time, and after the read-back Chrome converts it to CPU raster, with different antialiasing → different centroid colors → different triangle colors; ② more importantly, the offscreen layer's context state (`lineJoin='round'` set in facetize, `lineCap='round'` in segs) persists into the next frame, while the first frame uses the default miter/butt. Fix: create your own layer with `getContext('2d',{willReadFrequently:true})`, and **at the start of every frame call `g.reset()` (clears pixels + clears state) instead of `clearRect`**. Only found by hash-comparing "the same lt rendered three times in a row".
- With facet cell 34 the body is covered in small triangles and looks like a low-poly 3D model rather than Cubism; 58 is what gives "large planes of light and shade".
- The second, finer hair cut initially used a circular clip, which turned half the face gold too — fixed by adding the color filter `only`.

## Still improvable
- The girl's overall contour is still RIG's realistic curve; facetizing only changed the edges. A more thorough approach would replace the shoulders, elbows and knees with a polyline skeleton, then cut.
- The original's palette is darker with stronger value contrast (more large dark-brown planes); my version is on the bright side.
