# 55_dunhuang · Dunhuang (敦煌) mural (Northern Dynasties): mineral colors and flying lines on a dry mud wall

Status: research recipe; v1 demo added on 2026-10-16 (see "v1 implementation" at the end); self-review ~72, below 80, so it is not registered as a mature implementation.

## Before you start

Read [Cross-scene style distillation](../15-cross-scene-style-distillation.md) and the [doc 16 protocol](../16-style-demo-95-protocol.md) first, then this card. The original works are used only to compare visual language; figures, composition, and subject matter are not copied directly. The "stable features" below come from original works actually examined for this study, not official parameters.

## Scope

Choose one period before starting; do not mix the Northern Dynasties, High Tang, and Late Tang into a single "Dunhuang style":
- Northern Dynasties (Cave 257, *Jataka of the Deer King*; Cave 285, Western Wei apsaras; Cave 428, Northern Zhou): earth-red ground, white beasts, serrated mountains, blue-green wind leaves, apsaras flying horizontally. This card's v1 covers this branch.
- High Tang (Cave 112, *Pipa Played Behind the Back*; Cave 45, bodhisattvas): all-over gold and azurite, full-bodied figures, music-and-dance ensembles. Not yet made.

## Stable features (observed in original works)

- Ground: Cave 257 is an overall earth red in the #92342d family; Cave 285 is milk white #e8dcc5; the two can be split into upper and lower registers, with a multicolored horizontal band (black/white/blue/ochre) plus linked pearls between them.
- Form: beasts are a single white mass with a single outline and almost no interior lines; mountains are rows of narrow, tall serrated triangles, each painted one color and darkened on one side, with large areas of red ground exposed between the mountains; figures are small and spread horizontally.
- Apsaras (Cave 285): they fly horizontally, head first, with scarves streaming backward and the lower body turning into a long skirt trailing behind; small face, long slender brows and eyes, red blush on the cheeks; the linework uses red-brown iron-wire and orchid-leaf strokes.
- Wind leaves: comma-shaped blue-green curling leaves, turning backward in groups.
- Color: azurite #3e919c, malachite #577f7a, vermilion, ochre, lead white; lead white darkens with age and oxidation, and the skin in Cave 428 turns gray-blue.
- Wall surface: crackle, flaking paint that exposes the underlayer, salt efflorescence turning white, mud-wall granules; cracks run through the painted figures and beasts. A diamond-lattice band at the top and a hanging-curtain triangle band at the bottom.

## Difference from neighboring styles

Not Persian miniature painting (all-over filling, precision, gold lines); not Giotto (the white plaster layer of fresco); not Chinese-style (guofeng) illustration (smooth gradients and cloud patterns). Dunhuang's texture is "dry, old, cracked", and its colors are minerals, not paints.

## Production route (used for v1)

`scenes/55_dunhuang.js`: wall base (`P.texture` mud) → upper and lower registers + horizontal band + top diamond lattice + bottom hanging curtain (cached) → serrated mountains (cached) → motion: wind leaves turning left in layers by speed, apsaras (`apsara()`, widening ribbons `ribbon()`), scattered flowers, white deer running (`deer()`, two beats: airborne—gathered), hoof dust → overlay layer (crackle, flaking, salt efflorescence, placed over the painting) → mud-wall granules multiplied.

## v1 implementation (2026-10-16, self-review ~72, taste items pending Huashu, the author)

- Original anchoring: Cave 257 *Jataka of the Deer King*, Cave 285 Western Wei apsaras, Cave 428 Northern Zhou apsaras, Cave 112 *Pipa Played Behind the Back* (sources in the `reference.md` generated by `fetch_reference.py`; the Cave 112 and Cave 257 images are photo reproductions of composite plates, used only for palette and composition, not traced).
- Motion: wind leaves turning backward, apsaras bobbing up and down with ribbons swaying, scattered flowers falling, white deer and fawns running, hoof dust, drifting dust.
- Known shortcomings: the mountains are still too geometric-triangular and lack the layered, overlapping rhythm of Cave 257; the apsaras' torsos are too flat and lack the volume of a three-quarter turn; lead-white oxidation darkening has not been done; the High Tang branch has not been made.
- Not yet validated: cross-subject (replacing the apsaras with another subject), vertical format, and compositing with a voiceover film.
